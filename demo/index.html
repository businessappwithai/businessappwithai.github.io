Recording tips

  • Play the video with headphones on and record into any audio app as it plays. One take is easiest. If you stumble, redo just that block and cut it in.
  • Aim for the block to start when its timecode appears and finish before the next one. If a block runs long, trim a sentence rather than speeding up.
  • To add the audio to the video, run: ffmpeg -i best-crm-demo.mp4 -i voiceover.m4a -map 0:v -map 1:a -c:v copy -c:a aac -shortest best-crm-narrated.mp4
